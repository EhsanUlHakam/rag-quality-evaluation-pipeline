# LumenDesk Support Hours and Service Levels

Starter customers receive email support Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. Pro customers receive priority support 24 hours a day from Monday through Friday, with an eight-business-hour initial response target.

Enterprise support operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week. A Priority 1 Enterprise incident has a one-hour initial response target. Enterprise contracts include a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment; scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance is excluded from that calculation.
