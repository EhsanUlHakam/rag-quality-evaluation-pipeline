# LumenDesk Security and Data Retention

LumenDesk encrypts customer data with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Production access requires multi-factor authentication and is logged. Encrypted backups are retained for 35 days.

After a workspace is closed, its active customer data is scheduled for deletion within 30 days. Enterprise audit logs are retained for 12 months unless a different period is written into the customer contract. These retention periods do not replace a customer's legal or regulatory obligations.
