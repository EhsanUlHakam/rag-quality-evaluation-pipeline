# LumenDesk Subscription Plans

LumenDesk offers three subscription plans. The Starter plan costs $19 per user per month and includes shared workspaces, task boards, and email support. The Pro plan costs $49 per user per month and adds SAML single sign-on, advanced analytics, and priority support. Enterprise pricing is custom and includes SCIM provisioning, audit-log exports, and a named success manager.

Every new workspace receives a 14-day free trial. Annual subscriptions receive a 15% discount compared with monthly billing. Storage limits are 20 GB for Starter, 200 GB for Pro, and a contracted allowance for Enterprise.
