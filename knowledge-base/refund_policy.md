# LumenDesk Refund Policy

Monthly subscription payments are non-refundable, although customers may cancel renewal at any time. A first annual subscription purchase is eligible for a full refund when the request is submitted within 30 calendar days of purchase. Annual renewals are not refundable.

Customers request a refund by emailing billing@lumendesk.example from a workspace-owner account. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
