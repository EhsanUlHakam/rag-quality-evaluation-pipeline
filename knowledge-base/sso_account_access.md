# LumenDesk SSO and Account Access

SAML 2.0 single sign-on is available on Pro and Enterprise plans. Enterprise customers can also use SCIM to create, update, and deactivate user accounts. Multi-factor authentication can be enabled on every plan.

Five consecutive failed password attempts lock a local account for 15 minutes. A workspace owner can request an earlier unlock through support after identity verification. SSO users follow the lockout rules of their identity provider.
